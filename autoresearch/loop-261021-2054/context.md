# Experiment context

Autoresearch was installed during ongoing Hardest development. The earlier exploratory trials were not clear improvements:

- Development 1, seed 20261201, 100 paired seeds per setup: vs prior Hardest 52.5% whole teams and 50.5% with Medium partners; vs Harder 55.5% whole teams and 52.5% with Medium partners.
- Development 2, seed 20261202, 150 paired seeds with Medium partners: vs Harder 50.3333%. The isolated baseline reproduced its 151 wins in 300 matches.

These are development results used to choose experiments. They are excluded from final held-out inference. Fixed training matches use common deal sequences and swapped teams. Final settings will be frozen before the held-out run. Concurrent multiplayer and UI changes in the shared checkout are outside the experimental scope.
